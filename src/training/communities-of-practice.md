We encourage you to participate in professional communities to learn from others and share knowledge.

Examples include:

* NHSBSA User Research Community
* DDaT Communities of Practice
* Cross-Government User Research Community (via Slack)
* Government Digital and Data profession networks

This might involve:

* attending community sessions
* presenting at show and tells
* sharing research outcomes
* contributing to discussions
* helping develop templates, guidance and standards
