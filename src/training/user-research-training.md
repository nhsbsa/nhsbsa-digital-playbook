The UCD Leadership Team regularly shares training opportunities for the user research community.

Examples delivered during 2025/26 include:

* Introduction to User Research in Government
* Effective Mentoring
* Become a GDS Assessor
* Safeguarding and Personal Safety
* Research Ethics in User Research

You can also identify external learning opportunities, including:

* Udemy courses
* Skills Development Network
* specialist training providers
* conferences and events
* Government Digital and Data profession learning opportunities

You should agree any requests for external training with your user research lead and people manager.

You can find further information about [UCD training](https://nhsbsauk.sharepoint.com/sites/CapabilityandOperations/SitePages/UCD%20Training.aspx) on My Hub.
